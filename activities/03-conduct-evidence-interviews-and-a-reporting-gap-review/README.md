# Activity 3 — Conduct Evidence Interviews and a Reporting Gap Review

**Alignment:** K2 · K8 · A8 · LO1  
**Duration:** 50 minutes  
**Objective:** Gather, triangulate and record objective evidence, then convert confirmed gaps into clear audit findings.

## Scenario

Facilities says refrigerant data comes from service reports, Finance says all invoices were supplied, Procurement used spend factors without recording versions, and Marketing already drafted a reduction claim. The report describes freight and supplier inputs as primary data although several values are estimates. One acquisition was added to 2025 totals without revisiting the base year.

## Materials
- Interview role cards
- Invoices, meter extracts and service records
- Draft calculation and report extracts
- Evidence and findings workbook

## Questions
- Which statements require corroboration?
- What evidence is sufficient and reliable?
- Which gaps are nonconformities versus observations?
- How should sensitive emerging issues be communicated?

## Detailed procedure
1. Prepare open, probing and confirmation questions linked to the audit criteria and risks.
2. Conduct interviews without leading the interviewee and record factual answers separately from auditor evaluation.
3. Trace selected totals from report to model, source record, unit conversion, factor and approval.
4. Corroborate interview claims through records, observation or independent data.
5. Reperform selected calculations and document any difference, cause and potential consequence.
6. Evaluate evidence against the precise criterion and determine conformity, nonconformity, OFI or insufficient evidence.
7. Write each finding as criterion, objective evidence and clear evaluation without prescribing an unsupported solution.
8. Calibrate finding wording and significance with another audit team.
9. Prepare a concise emerging-issues briefing that preserves audit independence and factual accuracy.

## Required evidence
- Interview and corroboration records
- Source-to-report audit trail
- Recalculation samples
- Finding register and issues briefing

## Acceptance check

Important interview claims are corroborated; recalculations are reproducible; findings link criterion, evidence and evaluation; fact and judgement remain clearly separated.
