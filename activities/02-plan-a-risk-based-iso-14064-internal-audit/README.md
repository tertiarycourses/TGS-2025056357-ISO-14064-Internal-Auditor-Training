# Activity 2 — Plan a Risk-Based ISO 14064 Internal Audit

**Alignment:** K1 · K8 · A8 · LO1  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Objective:** Define audit objectives, scope, criteria, independence, risks, sampling and timetable for a two-day internal audit.

## Scenario

The audit covers the 2025 organisational statement and the Lumina 400 lifecycle footprint. High-risk areas include refrigerant leaks, electricity procurement claims, imported aluminium, outsourced warehousing, supplier data from China, use-phase assumptions and a proposed '30% lower-carbon' marketing claim. The Sustainability Manager prepared several calculations and cannot audit their own work.

## Materials
- Audit mandate and intended users
- Draft boundary and category totals
- Prior issues and organisational changes
- Risk and sampling workbook

## Questions
- What must the audit conclude?
- Which areas can materially affect the statement or user decisions?
- How will independence and competence be protected?
- Which populations and samples provide persuasive evidence?

## Detailed procedure
1. Write audit objectives that distinguish conformance, control effectiveness, data reliability and report readiness.
2. Define scope by entity, site, product, reporting period, categories, systems and locations.
3. Name criteria and record unresolved interpretation questions before fieldwork.
4. Assess inherent risk, control risk, data quality, magnitude, change and stakeholder sensitivity for each area.
5. Assign independent auditors and specialists; record conflicts and safeguards.
6. Define populations and sample logic using high-value, unusual, high-risk and representative items.
7. Allocate time to interviews, observation, source testing, recalculation and daily team calibration.
8. Prepare interview themes and an opening-meeting agenda covering logistics, evidence access and communication.
9. Review whether planned procedures are sufficient to support each required conclusion.

## Required evidence
- Audit charter and scope
- Risk assessment
- Sampling plan
- Interview timetable and opening agenda

## Acceptance check

The plan links risks to procedures and samples, defines independence and competence, covers both statements without scope ambiguity and can support the planned conclusions.
