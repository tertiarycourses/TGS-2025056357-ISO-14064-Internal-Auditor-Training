# ISO 14064 Internal Auditor Training

WSQ courseware for **TGS-2025056357**, developed by Tertiary Infotech Academy Pte Ltd.

This release turns ISO 14064 requirements and internal-audit practice into a connected two-day case study. Learners work as HarbourLight Electronics' internal audit team and examine the Lumina 400 product and organisational GHG evidence from criteria selection through audit close-out.

## Courseware

- `courseware/ISO 14064 Internal Auditor Training-v3.0.pptx` — editable 138-slide trainer deck
- `courseware/ISO 14064 Internal Auditor Training-v3.0.pdf` — learner slide PDF
- `courseware/LG-ISO 14064 Internal Auditor Training.docx` and `.pdf` — detailed Learner Guide
- `LG-ISO 14064 Internal Auditor Training.md` — accessible Markdown Learner Guide
- `courseware/LP-ISO 14064 Internal Auditor Training.docx` and `.pdf` — two-day Lesson Plan
- `activities/` — ten self-contained activity folders with detailed Markdown/PDF instructions and editable Excel workbooks

The PowerPoint is concept-led. Detailed procedures, scenarios, questions, required evidence and acceptance criteria are contained in the Learner Guide and activity folders.

## Connected activities

1. Map the GHG System and Audit Criteria
2. Plan a Risk-Based ISO 14064 Internal Audit
3. Conduct Evidence Interviews and a Reporting Gap Review
4. Define Organisational and Product Lifecycle Boundaries
5. Build the GHG Source, Sink and Scope Register
6. Diagnose Hotspots and Design a Mitigation Roadmap
7. Construct the Lumina 400 Lifecycle Inventory
8. Quantify and Audit the Product Carbon Footprint Model
9. Evaluate Uncertainty and Product Benchmark Performance
10. Close the Audit and Issue a Verification-Ready Report

## Learning outcomes

- Develop process mappings and document GHG accounting reports aligned to ISO 14064 and international verification guidelines.
- Define lifecycle boundaries and determine emission sources to identify carbon reduction opportunities.
- Construct lifecycle inventory data using product configurations and scoped emission categories.
- Determine uncertainty factors and evaluate product carbon performance against industry benchmarks.
